Our commitment

We are committed to providing a website and patient experience that are accessible to as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

Accessibility features

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive controls
  • Visible focus indicators
  • Semantic headings and page structure
  • Responsive layouts for different screen sizes
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences where applicable
  • Form labels and clear validation messages
